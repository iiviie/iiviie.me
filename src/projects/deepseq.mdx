# deepseq

DeepSeq is a system used by marine biologists at the **Centre for Marine Living Resources and Ecology** (Ministry of Earth Sciences) to identify novel deep-sea marine species and taxonomy using **eDNA** (environmental DNA).

The project is actively used in research and was the work that won me **Smart India Hackathon** — India's largest hackathon, with over **1M+ student participants**.

## The Problem

The deep sea is one of the least-explored environments on Earth. Traditional species identification relies on physically capturing organisms, which is slow, expensive, and misses the vast majority of biodiversity. eDNA — genetic material organisms shed into their environment — offers a way to detect species from a simple water or sediment sample, but the sequencing data is noisy and many deep-sea species have no reference in existing databases.

## What DeepSeq Does

- Processes raw eDNA sequencing data into clean, analyzable signals
- Identifies known species by matching against reference databases
- Flags and clusters **novel** sequences that don't match anything known — surfacing candidate new species and taxonomy
- Gives marine biologists a workflow to go from a sample to insight without manual bioinformatics overhead

## Recognition

DeepSeq won **Smart India Hackathon**, India's largest hackathon (1M+ participants), and is actively used in research at the Centre for Marine Living Resources and Ecology under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
